# Singapore HYSA Guide 2026

If you're looking to grow your savings in Singapore, a High Yield Savings Account (HYSA) is one of the most accessible ways to earn competitive interest rates on your deposits. In 2026, the landscape remains competitive, with UOB, OCBC, DBS, and newer digital banks offering attractive effective interest rates (EIR) that can reach well above 3% p.a.

## What Is a High Yield Savings Account?

A HYSA is a savings account that offers higher interest rates compared to a standard savings account. In Singapore, most HYSAs are tiered — meaning the interest rate you earn depends on how much you save and whether you meet certain bonus criteria like salary crediting, card spend, or bill payments.

## How Tiered Interest Works

Banks in Singapore typically structure their HYSAs with base interest plus bonus tiers. For example, UOB One offers bonus interest when you credit your salary and spend at least S$500 on your UOB card each month. OCBC 360 has similar tiers for salary, save, spend, insure, and invest categories.

Each category adds a bonus rate, and when combined, your effective interest rate can be significantly higher than the base rate. The key is understanding which categories fit your lifestyle so you can optimise without changing your spending habits.

## Understanding Effective Interest Rate (EIR)

The EIR is the actual annual interest rate you earn after accounting for how interest compounds and which bonus tiers you qualify for. It's different from the nominal rate advertised. For instance, a bank might advertise "up to 3.6% p.a." but that top rate only applies if you meet all bonus conditions.

## Tips to Maximise Your Savings Interest in 2026

- **Credit your salary** — Almost every tiered HYSA requires salary crediting (typically S$1,600–S$2,000/month) for the highest bonus tier.
- **Use the bank's credit card** — Spend S$500–S$800 on the bank's card each month to unlock the spend bonus.
- **Pay bills through the account** — Some banks like OCBC 360 reward you for paying at least 3 bills monthly.
- **Grow your savings balance** — Higher balances unlock higher tiers. Most banks cap bonus interest at S$100,000–S$200,000.
- **Insure or invest** — OCBC and DBS offer additional bonus tiers for purchasing insurance or investment products.
