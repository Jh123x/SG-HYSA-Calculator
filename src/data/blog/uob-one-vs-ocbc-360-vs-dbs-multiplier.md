# UOB One vs OCBC 360 vs DBS Multiplier

UOB One, OCBC 360, and DBS Multiplier are Singapore's most popular high yield savings accounts. Each offers tiered bonus interest, but their requirements and effective rates differ significantly. In this comparison, we break down which account earns more based on your financial habits. Find the latest rates on the official pages for [UOB One](https://www.uob.com.sg/personal/save/everyday-accounts/uob-one-account.page), [OCBC 360](https://www.ocbc.com/personal-banking/deposits/360-account), and [DBS Multiplier](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/deposits/bank-earn/multiplier).

## UOB One Account

**How it works:** UOB One offers bonus interest when you credit your salary (at least S$1,600/month) and spend S$500+ on your UOB One Card each month. You also earn bonus interest on the first S$150,000 of your savings, with multiple sub-tiers that apply progressively higher rates on each tier.

**Best for:** Salaried employees who already use UOB credit cards. The requirements are simple — just salary credit + card spend. Note that UOB also offers an alternative bonus tier via 3+ GIRO transactions in lieu of salary credit.

**Effective rate:** Rates vary based on your savings amount and which conditions you meet. Use our [SG HYSA Calculator](/) to get your personalised EIR based on your actual profile.

## OCBC 360 Account

**How it works:** OCBC 360 has five bonus categories — Salary (S$1,800+ credit), Save (increase your balance month-on-month by S$500+), Spend (S$500+ on OCBC card), Insure, and Invest. Each category you qualify for adds bonus interest, with the first S$75,000 earning one rate and the next S$25,000 earning a higher rate. Bonus interest is capped at S$100,000 balance.

**Best for:** Users who can meet multiple categories — especially those who want to earn extra by insuring or investing through OCBC. There is also a bonus for maintaining S$250,000+ in savings.

**Effective rate:** Rates depend on which bonus categories you qualify for. Check our calculator for your specific situation.

## DBS Multiplier Account

**How it works:** DBS Multiplier rewards you for transacting in multiple categories: Salary credit, Credit Card spend, Home Loan instalments, Insurance, and Investments. The more categories you use and the higher your total monthly transactions, the higher your interest rate multiplier. Rates follow a 3×3 grid based on transaction volume tier and number of categories.

**Best for:** DBS/POSB customers who already have multiple products with the bank — a home loan, insurance, or regular investments. Younger customers (under 30) can also get bonus interest without salary credit.

**Effective rate:** Rates vary based on eligible transaction volume and categories activated. Use our calculator to compare.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Feature | UOB One | OCBC 360 | DBS Multiplier |
| ------- | ------- | -------- | -------------- |
| Salary Requirement | S$1,600 | S$1,800 | No fixed minimum (salary counts toward eligible transactions) |
| Card Spend | S$500 | S$500 | S$500 (counts as 1 category) |
| Max Balance for Bonus Interest | S$150,000 | S$100,000 | S$100,000 (up to S$50,000 with 1 category) |
| Extra Bonus Categories | GIRO transactions (3+) | Save, Insure, Invest, S$250K+ balance | Home Loan, Insurance, Investments |
| Rate Structure | Progressive sub-tiers per tier | Additive bonuses per category | 3×3 grid by transaction volume & category count |

## Which Should You Choose?

- **Choose UOB One** if you want the simplest requirements — just salary credit + card spend. Ideal for anyone who already uses UOB cards. Also consider if you can do 3+ GIRO transactions instead.
- **Choose OCBC 360** if you're willing to meet multiple criteria. The Insure and Invest categories give OCBC an edge for those who already have insurance or investment products with the bank.
- **Choose DBS Multiplier** if you have a DBS home loan or multiple DBS products. The multiplier effect rewards deeper relationships, and the structure rewards higher transaction volumes.
