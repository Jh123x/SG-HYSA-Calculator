# UOB One vs OCBC 360 vs DBS Multiplier

UOB One, OCBC 360, and DBS Multiplier are Singapore's most popular high yield savings accounts. Each offers tiered bonus interest, but their requirements and effective rates differ significantly. In this comparison, we break down which account earns more based on your financial habits.

## UOB One Account

**How it works:** UOB One offers bonus interest when you credit your salary (at least S$1,600/month) and spend S$500+ on your UOB One Card each month. You also earn interest on the first S$100,000 of your savings.

**Best for:** Salaried employees who already use UOB credit cards. The requirements are simple — just salary credit + card spend.

**Effective rate:** Up to 3.00% EIR on the first S$100,000 when all conditions are met.

## OCBC 360 Account

**How it works:** OCBC 360 has five bonus categories — Salary (S$1,800+ credit), Save (increase your balance month-on-month), Spend (S$500+ on OCBC card), Insure, and Invest. Each category you qualify for adds bonus interest.

**Best for:** Users who can meet multiple categories — especially those who want to earn extra by insuring or investing through OCBC.

**Effective rate:** Up to 3.35% EIR when all categories are met, with bonus capped at S$100,000 balance.

## DBS Multiplier Account

**How it works:** DBS Multiplier rewards you for transacting in multiple categories: Salary credit, Credit Card spend, Home Loan instalments, Insurance, and Investments. The more categories you use, the higher your interest rate multiplier.

**Best for:** DBS/POSB customers who already have multiple products with the bank — a home loan, insurance, or regular investments.

**Effective rate:** Up to 3.00% EIR, with bonus tiers that increase based on the number of categories you activate.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Feature | UOB One | OCBC 360 | DBS Multiplier |
| ------- | ------- | -------- | -------------- |
| Salary Requirement | S$1,600 | S$1,800 | S$2,000 |
| Card Spend | S$500 | S$500 | S$500 |
| Max Balance Capped | S$100,000 | S$100,000 | S$100,000 |
| Max EIR | 3.00% | 3.35% | 3.00% |
| Extra Categories | None | Insure, Invest | Home Loan, Insurance, Invest |

## Which Should You Choose?

- **Choose UOB One** if you want the simplest requirements — just salary credit + card spend. Ideal for anyone who already uses UOB cards.
- **Choose OCBC 360** if you're willing to meet multiple criteria. The earn and insure categories give OCBC an edge in max EIR.
- **Choose DBS Multiplier** if you have a DBS home loan or multiple DBS products. The multiplier effect rewards deeper relationships.
